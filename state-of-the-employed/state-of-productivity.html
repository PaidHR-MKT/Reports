In simpler terms, imagine your paycheck shrinking over time. Inflation is like constantly having to spend more money on the same things you used to buy. This can be incredibly frustrating, as Kunmi, a 34-year-old Lagosian who earns 30,000 Naira monthly, can attest.
I had to reduce the amount I spend on feeding because of the high cost of food.
Kunmi (34, Single, 30k)

The Squeeze of Inflation

Savings and spending are not the only thing feeling the brunt of the economy, rising costs are also impacting motivation at work. 

30% of respondents reported feeling less productive compared to last year due to inflation. This suggests a concerning link between financial anxieties and work performance.

For example, Sam, a Lagos resident who earns 25,000 Naira monthly, exemplifies this connection.
I don’t eat at work till I get back home, that's why I don’t spend on food. Because I still have to do ajo.
Sam (82, Married, 25k)
